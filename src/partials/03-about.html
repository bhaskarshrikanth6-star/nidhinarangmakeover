About

A studio for the way you want to feel.

Nidhi Narang Makeovers is a makeup and beauty studio in Shahdara, East Delhi, led by Nidhi Narang. The work spans bridal makeup, hair, nails and skin.

Nidhi works with your features, your outfit and the occasion to create finishes that feel refined rather than heavy. Home service is available for those who prefer to get ready where they are.
